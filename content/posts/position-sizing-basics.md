Most traders obsess over entries. The durable edge usually lives in **how much** you risk when the setup appears.

Position sizing is the bridge between analysis and survival. A correct thesis with oversized exposure can still end the account. A mediocre setup with disciplined sizing leaves room to learn.

My baseline rules:

1. **Define risk per trade in capital, not conviction.** I decide the dollar amount I am willing to lose before looking at charts.
2. **Size from stop distance.** Wider invalidation means smaller share count — not a wider mental stop.
3. **Cap correlated exposure.** Multiple positions in the same macro theme are one bet until proven otherwise.

投资交易里，仓位管理比预测方向更重要。先活下来，再优化胜率。

This is written thinking, not financial advice — but the habit of sizing first has made my journal quieter and my decisions slower in a good way.
